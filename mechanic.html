Frequently asked questions

What do you repair on-site, and what requires the shop?
On-site: diagnostics, brakes, battery, sensors, minor electrical/mechanical jobs, oil and filter changes. Complex operations (timing systems, clutch, major engine / transmission work) are done in the shop.
How long does a full diagnostic take?
Usually 30–40 minutes. Includes OBD scanning and manual system tests.
Do you provide warranty for labor and parts?
Yes. Labor is warranted; parts depend on the manufacturer (OEM/aftermarket).
Can you come in the evening or on weekends?
Yes, by appointment. Same-day service is sometimes available.
How do I pay?
Cash / card / transfer. We confirm the estimate before work starts.
